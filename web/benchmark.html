STEMPROOF / BENCHMARK
EVALUATION & TRANSPARENCY

Show the checks. Publish the limits.

A verification product must be tested against known answers and deliberately introduced defects. This page defines the initial benchmark protocol without inventing performance claims.

01 / POSITIVE CASES

Known-correct items

Test supported equations, equivalent expressions and physics calculations with independently established expected results.

02 / NEGATIVE CASES

Injected defects

Change answer keys, alter values and introduce malformed inputs. Confirm that the expected mismatch is flagged.

03 / BOUNDARY CASES

Unsupported content

Include ambiguous, incomplete and unsupported items. Confirm they are routed to review rather than marked verified.

RELEASE 1 BENCHMARK POLICY

No borrowed accuracy numbers.

The repository tests cover the implemented rules and API behavior. They are not a representative customer question-bank benchmark, a psychometric study or evidence of educational efficacy. Before marketing an accuracy figure, publish the test-set composition, ground-truth process, per-rule precision and recall, false-positive examples, and limitations.
