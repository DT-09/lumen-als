# VESPER — Product definition

## Central promise

Turn a question you care about into structured understanding, independent investigation, original work, and evidence you can explain.

## The learner journey

1. **Discover** — capture a question, why it matters, and what a useful answer would look like.
2. **Map** — break the question into concepts, prerequisites, uncertainties, and a sequence of work.
3. **Understand** — build explanations in your own words, connect concepts, and test retrieval.
4. **Investigate** — state a hypothesis, define variables or evidence criteria, record observations, and note limitations.
5. **Create** — produce a paper, model, simulation, code project, experiment plan, or technical explanation.
6. **Demonstrate** — defend the result, show evidence, identify what remains uncertain, and record transfer.
7. **Retain** — schedule concepts for deliberate revisit and record what changed after review.

## Product principles

- One coherent learner workspace, not a course catalogue.
- Questions and original work are the organising objects.
- The learner owns the route and can revise it.
- Evidence and uncertainty are visible.
- No fake mastery scores, streak pressure, public rankings, or engagement tricks.
- Local-first privacy and explicit export/import.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible, reduced-motion-friendly interface.

## Not part of this release

Accounts, cloud sync, AI tutoring, AI route generation, live web research, source verification, collaboration, social feeds, billing, and automated mastery certification.
